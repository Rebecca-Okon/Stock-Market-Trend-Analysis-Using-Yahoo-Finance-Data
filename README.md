Project 1: Stock Market Trend Analysis Using Yahoo Finance
Data

Dataset: Yahoo Finance (e.g., AAPL, TSLA, GOOGL)
Introduction:
Stock market analysis is crucial for investors to make informed decisions. This project involves
analyzing historical stock prices, identifying trends, and evaluating risk through statistical and
visual methods. Students will use Python to fetch, clean, and analyze stock data, applying key
financial indicators such as moving averages and Bollinger Bands.
Key Questions to Answer:
1. What is the overall price trend of the selected stock over the past 5 years?
2. How do the 50-day and 200-day moving averages compare, and what do they indicate?

3. What were the highest and lowest volatility periods, and what caused them?
4. Are there any significant support/resistance levels in the stock’s price history?
5. How does trading volume correlate with price movements?
   
Tasks to Perform:
● Use yfinance to fetch historical stock data.
● Clean data (handle missing values, outliers).
● Calculate and plot moving averages.
● Implement Bollinger Bands for volatility analysis.
● Generate candlestick charts and volume trend plots.

Deliverables:
● Jupyter Notebook with complete Python code.
● Report with insights, visualizations, and conclusions.
● Interactive plots (Plotly/Matplotlib).
